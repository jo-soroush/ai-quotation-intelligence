# Claude Code entry point

Read `AGENTS.md` completely, then `PROJECT_CONTROL.md` and the applicable
canonical Card specification and evidence. Follow the Harness STOP conditions.

When assigned the INDEPENDENT VERIFIER role, review the frozen candidate from
the specification first. Read its Acceptance Contract and Critical Invariants,
inspect the diff and executed evidence, and report findings with reproducible
references. Do not certify from implementation-agent claims or edit the
candidate during audit. Remediation needs separate authorization; re-audit any
remediated candidate.

Do not commit, push, merge, authorize delivery, or start another Card.
